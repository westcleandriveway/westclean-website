Common Questions

Do I need to provide anything for the service?

For pressure washing, we just need access to an external water tap. We bring all our own professional-grade hoses, power equipment, and fuel. For lawn mowing, we are 100% self-sufficient.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not at all! As long as we have access to the areas being cleaned and a water tap, many of our customers are at work while we transform their property. We can send you "After" photos via WhatsApp once we finish.

Are you insured?

Yes. WestClean is fully insured, giving you total peace of mind while we are working on your property. We treat every home with the utmost care and respect.

How long does a typical driveway clean take?

Most standard driveways take between 1.5 to 3 hours depending on the size and the level of buildup. We don't rush—we make sure every stain is addressed before we pack up.
