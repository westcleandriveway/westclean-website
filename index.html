Common Questions

Do you need access to my water?

Yes, we connect to your external garden tap. Our equipment is water-efficient but requires a standard tap connection to provide professional pressure.

How long does a driveway take?

A standard single driveway usually takes 1-2 hours. Large bundles like the Home Hero can take 3-4 hours depending on the grime level.

Are you insured?

Absolutely. WestClean is fully insured for all residential and commercial property work, giving you total peace of mind.
